Body Cameras

Watch Video


Hytera Body Camera

Hytera with over 32 years experience in public safety is unwavering in meeting the demands of its customers. The Body Camera Solution with innovative hardware and software design, focus on recording of law enforcement and evidence management, integrating with control center solution, and convergencing with push-to-talk technology. It provides professional and efficient services of evidence management and visualized dispatch to Law Enforcement, Jail, Customs, Private Security and other industries.

Discover Your Ideal Body Camera Solution

Bodycam, the Witness of the Truth

The Body Camera as a mobile recorder, captures video evidence in both daily operation and critical situations, helping backtrack on the truth. For law enforcement, high-quality video saves the cost of resolving complaints, improving transparency to further strengthen police-citizen relationship. For the other industries who need to obey the SOP and face possible conflicts, it helps oversee strict operation processes and reduce liability. With full range of body camera product portfolio, we match you with the best solution to meet your needs and budget.

One-stop Evidence Management Services

Gather together and manage your captured evidence in a unified way, uploading them to Digital Evidence Management Platform (DEM) through a secure and reliable path. Powerful features facilitate you to achieve efficient evidence retrieval, case investigation, statistical analysis and other services in a huge database.

Stay In Touch with the Scene

Not just a recorder. Cooperated with Hytera HyTalk Solution, the body camera can establishes visual access between the field and command center. Live-streaming, live-location and other dispatch services allow you to make an accurate respond, and provide timely support in the field.

Get a All-in-one Radio for Both Video and Communication

Modern body cameras are smaller, lighter, and increasingly comprehensive in the type of features they now support. In addition to excellent video and audio recording capabilities, Hytera Smart Body Cameras equipped with large PPT button, achieves audio call, video call and other communication services with Hytera HyTalk app over LTE, or connects to a PMR radio as an RSM (remote speaker microphone) use.

Key Benefits

Capturing Every Detail

Capture ultra-clear and authentic footages as the incident unfolds, helping trace back the truth. Even in emergency situations, it provides a super-long pre-recording buffer to record the entire event. High-power infrared lights ensure clear recording in full darkness.

Instant Eyes-on-scene

View live video and GPS location of body camera for greater situation awareness, ensuring timely and accurate decision-making. For law enforcement, the body camera solution helps coordinate the resources and personnel to ensure the security and stability of each city.

Push to Talk

Besides video services, the body camera delivers fast and secure PTT voice communication at the press of the PTT key. Bring loudness and voice clarity into every conversation even in busy streets or transportation hubs, by an collaboration of high-power speakers and the AI-based noise cancellation algorithm.

Always Ready for Next Shift

One body camera can be easily used by multiple people and meet the needs of shifts. Just tapping the RFID card to the body camera, you can switch your ID information and effectively track the source of evidence. During battery replacement, its built-in backup battery ensures an uninterrupted recording for 3 to 5 minutes, seamlessly extending the battery life.

Secure the Evidence

The whole body camera solution, in terms of technology and management, ensures that digital evidence are not tampered with or leaked, from being captured, uploaded, to being stored and managed by the DEM platform. It truly guarantees the authenticity and admissibility of digital evidence.

High Quality Manufacturing

Boast an ultra-long service life — lying in the industrial-grade components and professional manufacturing. Rugged, reliable, and ready to face rough handling day after day. Combat dust, sand, water, and even extreme temperatures — giving you the confidence to get the job done anywhere.
