Digital Evidence Management Platform

Cloud-native Digital Evidence Management (DEM) is a rich-featured platform designed to collect, store, analyze and process huge volumes of digital evidence. It seamlessly integrates a variety of media types,including videos, audios, photos, and documents from body cameras or other third-party sources. The DEM supports both on-premise and cloud deployment, allowing you to create a unified media database with high security and reliability, thereby transforming evidence management into a digital and intelligent process.

Highlights

Efficient Evidence Storage

Hytera's Digital Evidence Management (DEM) system ensures that all captured data is securely uploaded and stored in a centralized repository.Utilizing the industry-leading database, it accelerates queries across hundreds of millions of complex, massive records, swiftly presenting target information.

Make Investigation More Efficient

Hytera DEM system enhances investigative efficiency by providing tools for categorizing, tagging, and linking evidence to specific cases. Investigators can quickly access the timeline of events, movement tracks, key video and audio clips, streamlining the review process and enabling faster resolution of cases.

Copies Redaction Protects Privacy

When evidence needs to be made public for media or trials, it is necessary to follow the needs of privacy protection as well as desensitization. Keeping the original file unaltered. Hytera DEM renders copies after redaction, such as video clipping, silencing. Professional Ul and functional design make it friendly for users to get started. Evidence copies redaction is no longer typically a slow and expensive process.

Intergrity of evidence chain

The integrity of evidence chain is the premise that digital evidence can be used as "evidence". Hytera DEM records every action taken by anyone around evidence as logs, generating audit reports automatically in proof. It maintains the chain of custody, which is essential for legal admissibility and trustworthiness.

Secure and reliable system

Hytera DEM is designed with system security and reliability at its core, leveraging a mature micro-services architecture that enhances stability and performance. It supports a buyout option, enabling customers to host their databases independently. This is the commitment to security and reliability in managing sensitive digital evidence, especially for public safety users.

Device Management

Hytera DEM also includes robust device management capabilities of body cameras and docking stations in the whole solution. It allows administrators to monitor the status and configuration of connected devices, ensuring they are functioning correctly. Diversified remote management gives you the flexibility to adjust or upgrade solutions securely; everything is under control.

Hytera SC780 Smart 4G Body Camera

Hytera SC780 Smart 4G Body Camera is a robust 4G body camera designed for law enforcement and public safety. It offers a 130° wide-angle view, 6-axis stabilization for clear video, and records 120 seconds before and after activation. With real-time video streaming and integration with DMR/TETRA radios, it ensures reliable communication. The SC780 is also durable, with IP68 water and dust resistance and a 3,200mAh battery for extended use.
