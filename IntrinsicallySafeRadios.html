Hytera Intrinsically Safe Radio & Smartphone

Hytera is committed to creating Intrinsically Safe (IS) radios and smartphones, also called explosion-proof walkie-talkies, for hazardous environments. With over 30 years of expertise, Hytera offers DMR and TETRA IS radios and PoC (Push-to-Talk over Cellular) smartphones. These devices are certified by IECEx, ATEX, and UL913, ensuring safety and reliability in hazardous work environments. Hytera's global sales network makes it easy for users worldwide to access EX radios, helping industries like oil & gas, mining, fire & rescue and other high-risk work sectors.

DMR & TETRA Intrinsically Safe Radios

DMR & TETRA IS radios are meticulously designed for hazardous environments. Featuring explosion-proof, dust-proof, and waterproof capabilities, they ensure clear and reliable voice communication in the most challenging conditions, such as flammable and explosive areas. These radios are essential in high-risk industries like oil and gas, fire and rescue, and mining, providing workers with enhanced safety and dependable communication.

Keep Safety for Your Teams

Ensuring safety in hazardous environments requires specialized equipment. Hytera IS radios are designed with advanced features to mitigate risks and ensure reliable communication. Empower your team and maintain continuous, clear communication in hazardous environments to improve safety and operational efficiency.

With a wider operating temperature range (in Ex areas), Hytera IS radio is suitable for diverse flammable and explosive atmospheres. Ensure continuous and reliable communication and operation.

AI-based Noise Cancellation for Clarity Voice

Hytera’s IS radio incorporates the most advanced AI-based noise cancellation algorithm and leverages machine learning. Trained with thousands of noise samples, IS radio can swiftly differentiate human voices from background noise. This ensures that workers receive clear commands from the very first word, enhancing communication accuracy and reducing the risk of misunderstandings, thereby improving overall safety and productivity.

Extended Radio Range for Long Distance Wireless Communications

In areas lacking signal coverage, remote suburbs, and regions with weak factory network infrastructure, Hytera IS radios exhibit extended coverage and stronger signal penetration. Whether it's offshore oil rigs, mines, and chemical processing plants, they seamlessly traverse distances and obstacles to enable long-range wireless communication.

PoC Intrinsically Safe Smartphone

PoC (Push-to-Talk over Cellular) IS smartphone stands out as a high-performance communication tool leveraging public mobile networks. It incorporates advanced technology to ensure crystal-clear and stable transmission of voice, video, and data, supporting multimedia capabilities. With robust explosion-proof construction, it resists dust, water, snow, and sudden drops. This makes it excel in environments demanding extensive coverage, high-speed communication, and rich multimedia functionality. Ideal sectors include oil and gas, pharmaceuticals, and food processing, where reliable and resilient communication is paramount.

High Reliability

PoC IS smartphone excels in safety with special explosion-proof materials that resist static electricity, sparks, and other hazardous elements, ensuring safe operation in combustible environments. Designed for durability, it withstands impact and vibration while featuring waterproof, dustproof, and shockproof characteristics. This reliability ensures stable communication in harsh environments, contributing to safer production and enhancing the personal safety of frontline workers.

Clear Visual Insights

PoC IS smartphone supports instant transmission of high-definition images, ensuring recipients can clearly view the transmitted visual content. Additionally, through real-time video streaming, the PoC IS smartphone allows recipients to gain immediate insights into on-site situations, providing more accurate and timely information support for decision-making.

Intrinsically Safe Smart Device

PNC460U

The PNC460U is a UL913-certified intrinsically safe smart device with the capabilities of large-screen smartphone and push-to-talk over cellular (PoC) radio rolled into one. It provides purpose-built apps, instant PoC calls, video-based dispatch, and more to get things done in less time and with less hassle. Work safer. Work more efficient.
