Why Partner with Hytera?

Hytera keeps delivering industry leading, end-to-end communications solutions to the market with our partners. Hytera's commitment to delivering value to clients is backed by working with best-in-class, qualified partners that help millions of professional users meet their diverse requirements across multifaced industries. We are proud of our strong partner relationships and are continually seeking to develop and expand them. Hytera's Partner Program provides a framework for partners to develop and leverage integrated communications solutions for their clients - from project support, product solution and purchase, to deployment and after sales service.

Your Goals, Our Mission

Hytera offers its Partners better commercial terms, training, joint marketing, and limitless cooperation opportunities: giving you the resources to grow your business, less pain, more gain!

Hytera Global Recruitment Program

Across the world, Hytera has worked and is working with thousands of business partners to deploy communication solutions for a user base of more than 15 million.

Benefits for Your Business

Profitability

We offer incentives, rebates, and support to partners and facilitate your growth of revenue and profit. Depending on your business model, you can be a Hytera products reseller or a system integrator to include Hytera equipment into your solution.

Accelerating Growth

Hytera's Partner Enabling Plan ensures partners get the support they need to drive growth. By partnering with us, you can leverage Hytera's lead generation campaigns and other advantages, including access to best-in-class technical training, marketing, and after-sales support.

Business Drivers

Enjoy our full range of technical resources, product demos, pilot systems, as well as the continuum of new product releases.

Respond and Achieve

Known for our rapid and effective response, we pride ourselves in standing with our partners and providing solutions to challenges. We're a trusted partner in the always changing and dynamic markets.


The Hytera Partner Recruitment Program encompasses Channel Sales Partners and Vertical Industry Partners, uniting efforts to distribute and customize professional communication solutions. Channel Sales Partners collaborate closely with Hytera to market and sell products, receiving benefits like training and marketing support. Vertical Industry Partners focus on tailoring Hytera's technology to specific sectors, leveraging expertise to optimize professional communication solutions for industries such as public safety, oil and gas.

Certification Process

1

Application

Please prepare information and documents such as contacts and company certification materials and begin the application process.

2

Review

Upon receiving your application, Hytera will conduct a review of your application within three working days.

3

Cooperation

Once your application is approved, you will be notified via email that you have become an official Hytera partner, and you'll have immediate access to our range of benefits, such as discounted purchasing, online training, and assigned leads.
